PROCESS LIFECYCLE:
0. Proposed (Draft PR submitted)
1. Under Review (Active community & sub-team discussion)
2. Final Comment Period (FCP - 10 day window)
3. Accepted (Merged into RFC tree; awaiting/under implementation)
4. Stabilized (Feature verified and enabled in stable compiler)
5. Closed/Deferred (Superseded or withdrawn)
